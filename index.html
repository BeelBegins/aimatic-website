What we do

Two things, done properly: getting ERPNext right for your business, and building the point-of-sale software that runs on top of it.

⚙

ERPNext implementation

Setup, data migration, custom workflows, and ongoing support for ERPNext deployments — accounting, inventory, purchasing, and retail operations configured around how your business actually runs.

🖥

Custom POS & software development

Point-of-sale and business software built to your requirements, integrated directly with your ERPNext instance — no bolted-on middleware, no duplicated data.

🧾

Tax & compliance integration

FBR e-invoicing and local tax compliance wired directly into the sale flow, so returns and audits are backed by data that was correct at the point of sale.

🔐

Secure device & staff access

Per-cashier authentication, one-time device enrollment for Android terminals, and role-based access — so ERPNext's permissions are what actually govern the till, not a shared password.
